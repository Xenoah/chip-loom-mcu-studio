# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to each other. Assume the person you are replying to is competent and
acting in good faith, and write as though they will read it twice.

## Expected behaviour

* Keep criticism on the work, not the person. "This will deadlock when the probe
  disconnects" is useful; "this is sloppy" is not.
* Say what you actually verified. "I tested this on a NUCLEO-G431KB" and "I only
  read the code" are both fine answers; conflating them is not.
* Accept that people work at different paces, in different time zones, in
  languages that are not their first. English and Japanese are both welcome in
  issues and pull requests.
* When you are wrong, say so and move on. Nobody is keeping score.

## Unacceptable behaviour

* Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
* Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
* Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
* Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberately derailing a review.

## Scope

This applies in every project space -- issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages -- and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the repository maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/Xenoah). Reports are handled privately. Maintainers
will explain a moderation decision to the person affected by it, and may remove
content, close discussions, or block an account.

Maintainers who do not follow this document are subject to it like anyone else.

メンテナへ非公開でご連絡ください。
